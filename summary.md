# 🦿 Captain Clubby – Medical Summary

---

## 🧬 Core Diagnoses & Concerns

- **Congenital Rigid Clubfoot (left)** – 3 surgeries, progressive deformity
- **Critical Limb Ischemia** – 2 of 3 arteries nonfunctional in left leg
- **Gout** – Chronic, uric acid 10.8, unmanaged with current treatment
- **Hashimoto’s Thyroiditis** – TSH 390, Free T4: 0.58, Free T3: 1.62
- **Hypertension** – Chronic, poorly controlled, exacerbated by pain/stress
- **Vitamin D Deficiency** – 17.1 ng/mL
- **Hammertoe Deformity** – Failed left toe correction; new right foot pain developing
- **Suspected Malabsorption** – Delayed medication effect, possible GI involvement
- **Elevated Liver Enzymes** – ALT: 108, AST: 76
- **Severe Mobility Limitation** – Cane-dependent, walking distance limited
- **Pain & Fatigue** – Chronic, mentally and physically debilitating

---

## 🏥 Treatment History

- Premature birth (1990); NICU care
- Multiple surgeries for left foot (latest in 2009 – full reconstruction)
- Foot deformity recurred; toes collapsed; function deteriorated
- Began cane use 3 years ago; mobility worsening
- Hospitalized 2025: confirmed blocked arteries; ischemic pain

---

## 🎯 Medical Goals

- **Below-knee amputation of left leg**
  - Due to progressive failure, deformity, and pain
- Establish coordinated care:
  - **Endocrinology** (thyroid & malabsorption)
  - **Rheumatology** (gout & inflammation)
  - **Vascular surgery** (amputation)
  - **PM&R** (prosthetic planning)
  - **Pain management**
